Purpose

The purpose of this specification is to provide a structured coating recommendation for each inspected surface included within this quotation. The document should be used together with the official quotation and manufacturer product data sheets.

General Application Notes

  • Ensure all surfaces are clean, dry and free from contaminants before coating.
  • Moisture levels should fall within manufacturer recommendations before application.
  • All preparation work should be completed before primers or finish coats are applied.
  • Observe all manufacturer drying and recoat times. Application should only be carried out under suitable environmental conditions.
